Projects

For now, the only project I have completed is my own website featuring a room-building game, which I created to gain practical experience with development.
The project allowed me to apply the knowledge I had gained and learn more about development in a hands-on way.
Since I am still an IT Architecture student, I am currently focusing on my studies and working on personal projects in my free time.
As I continue to learn and grow as a developer, I plan to work on more projects in the future,
both to further develop my skills and to create something that others can enjoy and interact with.

The room-building game website is currently the only project I have completed, but I am excited to continue learning and building more projects in the future.
Due to my inexperience, I received a lot of help from programmer friends abroad, who guided me through the process and helped me overcome challenges along the way.
I am grateful for their support and guidance, which allowed me to complete the project and gain valuable experience in development.

The room-building game is a web application that allows users to create and customize their own virtual rooms and avatars,
based on the popular Habbo Hotel game that was popular in the early 2000s.
The site is primarily based on PHP, with JavaScript and HTML/CSS for the front-end.
Huge credits goes to my programmer friends abroad, Justin and Revue, who helped me with the project and provided guidance and support
throughout the development process.
